Research

I've been in Dr. Jop van Berlo's lab at the Lillehei Heart Institute since November 2025. Adult hearts barely heal after injury, and most of what I do is about understanding why.

Macrophages in a growing heart

My own subproject, approved for directed research. Macrophages are immune cells that live in heart tissue. I'm comparing how they multiply when the heart grows normally against when it remodels after damage. I give mice EdU and then BrdU, which mark cells each time they copy their DNA, so I can tell which macrophages divided more than once. I'm also looking at how monocytes coming in from the blood change that picture.

Most days that means cutting frozen heart sections, staining them, a lot of antibody troubleshooting, imaging, and counting. I also grow macrophages from bone marrow.

How the heart matures

I stained heart tissue from different stages of development and measured how big the muscle cells were at each one. That went into this paper:

Mikkila JL, Clark AP, Wang R, Torniainen AA, Souza-Neto F, Yu T, et al., van Berlo JH. High-temporal analysis identifies the heart matures in four distinct phases. Nature Cardiovascular Research, under review, 2026.

WWC2 and Hippo–YAP signaling

A project I started myself. The Hippo–YAP pathway is part of what stops adult heart muscle cells from dividing. I'm trying to find what controls it upstream, using where YAP sits inside the cell as the readout. After the cell experiments, I moved to mice: I do the heart attack (myocardial infarction) surgeries and the miRNA injections myself.

Summer 2026, Beijing

From May to August I was a visiting researcher in Dr. Yuxuan Guo's lab at Peking University's Institute of Cardiovascular Science. I helped design engineered molecular tools aimed at LMNA-related cardiomyopathy, an inherited heart muscle disease, and tested them with cloning, PCR, Western blots, flow cytometry, and RNA extraction.

Funded in part by a $1,800 Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program award. Dean's List, 2025. I'm also trained on the scanning electron microscope.
